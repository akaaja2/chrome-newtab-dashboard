## Voyage of the Damned: format decision

- Episodic: 150k / 20k = 7.5, so either 8 episodes of ~18,750 words, or 7 episodes of 20,000 plus a 10,000-word finale.
- Novella: release as one book. Note that 150k words is novel length, not novella length, so "novella" here likely means a single volume.
- Decide this first. Editing, covers and release cadence all depend on it.
